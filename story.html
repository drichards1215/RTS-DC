The Story

✦

Every day, thousands of Washingtonians walk past the “great men and women” of history, among them Admiral David G. Farragut, President Benito Juárez, inventor Guglielmo Marconi and nurse Jane Delano, without ever noticing them.

Romancing the Stone stops to look. Where art meets history, it revisits the lives behind D.C.’s statues and monuments to find what they say about our values, then and today.

This site is your field guide. Find every statue on the Map, look one up in the Catalog, read photos and field notes in the Journal, and set out on the Quest to visit each one and earn its badge. Then share what you find with the Community.
